Support

Questions or feedback? Email leaf1979yezi@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 working days.

FreeTV 自带频道吗？/ Does FreeTV include channels?

不带。FreeTV 只是播放器，请添加你有权使用的服务商提供的 M3U、Xtream Codes 或 Stalker 播放列表。
No. FreeTV is a player only. Add an M3U, Xtream Codes or Stalker playlist from a provider you are authorised to use.

某个频道播不了 / A channel won't play

先在「工具 › 检测直播源」里检测一下。显示失效通常是服务商那边的问题。FreeTV 使用系统播放器，支持 HLS（m3u8）、MP4、MOV；部分原始 TS 直播流和 MKV 文件暂不支持。
Run Tools › Check streams first; a failing result usually means the provider's stream is down. FreeTV uses the system player, which supports HLS (m3u8), MP4 and MOV. Some raw TS live streams and MKV files are not supported yet.

检测结果大量失效 / Many streams show as failing

很多服务商限制同时连接数。请在「设置 › 高级」里把「检测并发数」调低（例如 2–4）再试。
Many providers limit simultaneous connections. Lower Settings › Advanced › Validation concurrency (e.g. 2–4) and try again.

试用与订阅 / Trial and subscription

7 天免费试用无需付款，到期不会自动扣费，可添加 1 个播放列表。FreeTV Pro 订阅可在 iPhone「设置 › Apple 账户 › 订阅」中管理或取消。换手机后请点「恢复购买」。
The 7-day trial needs no payment and never converts into a paid plan automatically; it includes 1 playlist. Manage or cancel FreeTV Pro in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. On a new device, tap Restore purchases.

iCloud 同步没反应 / iCloud sync isn't working

请确认已登录 iCloud，并在 iPhone「设置 › Apple 账户 › iCloud」中允许 FreeTV 使用 iCloud。打开或关闭同步开关后需要重启 App。
Make sure you're signed in to iCloud and FreeTV is allowed under Settings › Apple Account › iCloud. Restart the app after toggling sync.

网页管理器打不开 / Can't open the web manager

电脑和手机需要连同一个 Wi-Fi，并在 iPhone「设置 › 隐私与安全性 › 本地网络」中允许 FreeTV。
Your computer and phone must be on the same Wi-Fi, and FreeTV must be allowed under Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network.
